## How to credit and frame

- The detailed nuclear-detonation steps (shelter in minutes, lie face down, 24 hours) are **US
  guidance** (Ready.gov/FEMA, CDC). Say so on the card ("แนวทางของสหรัฐฯ กรณีระเบิดนิวเคลียร์").
- The Thai authority for radiation emergencies is **สำนักงานปรมาณูเพื่อสันติ (ปส.)**, hotline
  **1296** (24 h) / 0 2596 7600; its booklet says to follow government announcements and leave a
  shelter only when the government confirms it is safe. Disasters in general: **ปภ. 1784**.
- No Thai source mentions potassium iodide; CDC/WHO do.
- Narration must say only what the credited sources say — no extra slogans presented as advice.

## Verified facts (page = source table numbers in the research file)

| # | Fact | Credit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | When warned: get inside the nearest building immediately; move away from windows; put as many walls as possible between you and outside. | Ready.gov (/radiation) · CDC Get Inside · WHO ("Get in, stay in, tune in") |
| F2 | Best spot: basement or the middle of the building, away from walls and roof; brick/concrete multi-storey buildings safest; underground garages and subways also good shelter. Any building is safer than outside. | Ready.gov · CDC |
| F3 | After a detonation there may be 10 minutes or more before fallout arrives — use minutes, not hours (number is Ready.gov's only). | Ready.gov |
| F4 | Outdoors at the blast: turn away, cover your eyes, lie face down with hands under the body until the heat and the two shock waves pass; then go into the nearest building quickly. | CDC Nuclear Blasts FAQ · Ready.gov ("lie face down") |
| F5 | Stay inside at least 24 hours; leave earlier only for an immediate hazard (fire, gas leak, collapse, serious injury) or when authorities say it is safe. | Ready.gov · CDC Stay Inside |
| F6 | Do not go outside to fetch family; schools and hospitals have their own plans. | CDC About / Get Inside |
| F7 | Close windows and doors; switch off fans, air-conditioning and forced-air heating; bring pets inside. | Ready.gov · CDC |
| F8 | Follow official instructions by battery or hand-crank radio, TV, official social media. Thailand: ปส. 1296, ปภ. 1784. | CDC Stay Tuned · Ready.gov kit · ปส. booklet |
| F9 | Removing the outer layer of clothing can remove up to 90% of radioactive material; bag the clothes away from people and pets. | CDC Self-decontaminate · Ready.gov |
| F10 | Wash with soap and water, gently, no scrubbing or scalding; cover cuts; shampoo or soap for hair. ปส.: skin 2–3 min twice (or 2 min three times with a soft brush), hair 2 min three times; rinse eyes, ears, mouth; never swallow the rinse water. | CDC · ปส. booklet |
| F11 | Hand sanitizer and household cleaning wipes do not remove radioactive material from skin. | Ready.gov |
| F12 | Food/drink kept inside is safe; wipe sealed containers from outside; avoid garden produce and uncovered outdoor food until authorities say so. Boiling water does not remove radioactive material. | Ready.gov · CDC |
| F13 | Water for a kit: Ready.gov 1 gallon (≈3.8 L) per person per day (drinking + sanitation) for several days; Thai media citing ปภ.: 3 L per person per day for 72 h (secondary); ปภ. flood leaflet: supplies for 3–5 days. | Ready.gov · Thai PBS (secondary) · ปภ. leaflet |
| F14 | Potassium iodide: only when officials say; protects the thyroid only, not against fallout outside the body; iodized salt is no substitute. Timing differs (CDC 24 h before–4 h after; WHO up to 2 h after, 8 h still reasonable). | CDC · WHO |
| F15 | Kit items: water, food, radio, torch, first aid, batteries, whistle, dust mask, plastic sheeting + tape, wipes, rubbish bags, tools, can opener, maps, phone + power bank. | Ready.gov Build a Kit |
| F16 | Family plan: a familiar meeting place; know how to contact each other if separated. | Ready.gov Make a Plan |

Do **not** teach (not on any official page read): "never use conditioner", "iodized salt or
seaweed protects you", "boil water to make it safe", shelter "48 h / 3 days / 14 days", "don't
look at the flash" as a separate rule (CDC says "turn away, cover your eyes"), any Thai KI advice.
